# Flattening of Effect (WIP)

*Oct 20, 2026*
In preparation for turning twenty, I have recently been thinking about the possible lives I can live. Importantly, *what kind of life do I want to live*. I have found this hard to do on concrete terms: "mechanical engineer" 
"SWE" "Product designer". Not because I don't think these things are jobs that I could have, and thrive in, but because I feel the product designer of today will look very different from the product designer of 2030. Would I even like the new job? Who's to tell.

As I usually do when confronted like this, I abstract. What will have to stay the same? What about my interests project well onto these jobs in the present? So when presented with the question of what I want to do, I usually speak in prose about my aspirations and my confusing major. I think one of my aspirations is to do something that I enjoy, is notable, and seen as a gift to humanity. This is quite a big and rare thing to achieve, and can take many forms. As such, I have been trying to maximize the range of possible lives I could have. Maximize my propensity for heights.

Im sure this is what the angry mathematicians are concerned with too, aiming for notoriety and expanding the frontier.

Part of being American, I have learned while studying in Canada seems to be this urge for the frontier, to take risks to expand humanity's domain even when the unexplored space does not guarantee returns.

I suppose that I have fallen into this paradigm of optimization, which I've been planning on exploring here
