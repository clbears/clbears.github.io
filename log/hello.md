# Ad Astra per Aspera

*July 28, 2026*

What beautiful four words. I chose this as my motto while I still thought I wanted to become an aerospace engineer. Even though that goal was found personally hollow, these four words still ring true.

Ad Astra

I find, when I climb mountains, that the peak is not always the highlight or even a big payoff. The peak is a mere goal, a direction to focus your steps. Without the peak, there would be no hike. With no hike, there would be no joy. The joy comes from the intermediate views, the conversations you have with your friends, the feeling of the sublime.

per Aspera

There is no joy without suffering. Without hardship, we live a dull life. Hardship allows greatness to manifest via ambition. Hardship can make a dull experience ecstatic. 

Ad Astra per Aspera

Put together, you have the goal in which you can direct your steps and a recipe for a beautiful life.
