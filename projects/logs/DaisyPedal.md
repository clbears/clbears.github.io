## Pre Aug 16, 2026

The idea of this project is to take advantage of the Daisy Seed dev board to create a low-cost, high-utility guitar pedal.

As I do not play guitars at all, nor know how the scene is, I am doing this project with my friend, who provides the musical expertise.

As such, a lot of planning and consulting was involved and I did my best to bridge the gap between my technical knowledge and my friend's more abstract musical knowledge.
