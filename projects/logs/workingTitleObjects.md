## July 20, 2026
I made a thing that interfaces command strips and magnets to allow you to hang posters without direct adhesive. 

When I was searching for an alternate solution, I realized that so many things you find online to 3D print are ugly, gimicky, and generally not useful. I am currently outlining a design philosophy for a series of 3D models that are useful, beautiful, and honest. With the ability to create all of these beautiful forms, why do funtional models look like that? I am on the search for problems to solve.
