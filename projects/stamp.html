A stamp to make my mark. Printed in SLS nylon for APSC 100, carried on a red keychain cable with a knurled join.

The waist narrows into finger holds that set the grip and keep pressure square to the face. Forgery is handled by the process: sintering leaves a random grain no reprint repeats, so the mark protects itself.
