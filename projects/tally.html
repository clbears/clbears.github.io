Log
Build log, June 22 – August 25

Tally means a great deal to me, and I know I'll look back on it as my best work to date. It was made for one person, and I tried to account for every experience they might have with it, then shape each one.

Most of the time went into the click: how it sits in the palm, how it takes a slap on a desk. The weight, the lever, the thickness, all of it decided. I hope that years from now, stripped of all emotional significance, it stands on its own. A lone soldier, dutifully following orders from another time.

What makes a click worth repeating?
How do you make something that outlasts the reason it was made?
